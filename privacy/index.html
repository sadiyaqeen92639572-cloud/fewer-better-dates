Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-19. Plain-language summary of what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist (on the homepage or a city page), we collect: your email address, the qualification answers you fill in (age range, gender, who you're looking to meet, relationship goal, and what frustrates you about dating apps), which city page you signed up from, and, if present in the link you arrived from, UTM campaign parameters. We do not ask for or store your name, phone number, photo, or any payment details.

How it's stored

Waitlist data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database that only we have access to. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We use it to understand which cities show real interest and to email people once founding memberships open, nothing else.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The only client-side data handling is reading UTM parameters from the page URL (if present) to attribute a signup to a campaign, and this never touches third-party trackers or ad networks.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your waitlist entry at any time by contacting us via the waitlist form on the homepage and requesting removal; we'll delete the corresponding row from our database. If you're in the UK or EU, this is your right under UK GDPR / EU GDPR; if you're in the US, most states now provide an equivalent right to deletion.

Who runs this

Fewer Better Dates is published by Gesmine-Invest Limited, a UK-registered company (company number 14120136). See our About page for more on what this project is at this stage.
