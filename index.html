The problem

Dating apps give you more choice. Not necessarily better dates.

You swipe. You match. You chat. Then nothing happens.

If you're tired of dating apps, that's a fair read of how they're built: most of them make money when you keep swiping, so getting you a relationship and off the app was never the priority.

Or you meet someone who wants something completely different from what you do, and only find out three messages in. Dating apps don't sort for that upfront, so you end up doing the sorting yourself, badly, over and over.

We built something that sorts for it first.

The concept

Less swiping. More meeting.

Over 8 weeks, we introduce you to a small number of people based on your values, your goals and how you actually live, on top of the usual photos and shared interests.
