The honest take

Is Norwich good for serious dating?

Norwich is a genuine unknown for a dating product like this: the demographic mix (young-skewing, high never-married share, small enough to build real recurring community) fits the "intentional dating" thesis well on paper, but it's untested compared to somewhere like London. Worth watching as an affinity signal precisely because it isn't an obvious volume play.
