What the numbers say about dating in Manchester

Manchester's median age (31) is noticeably younger than London's, and its never-married share (55.8%) is one of the highest of any major English city: a long way above the England and Wales average of 37.9%. The city's dating culture leans heavily on its music, sport and student-city social scene rather than the more work-first pace of London.

The honest take

Is Manchester good for serious dating?

Manchester's combination of a young population and a high never-married share means the raw dating pool skews toward people who are, demographically, still actively looking rather than settled. That's good news for volume, but the same youth-and-churn dynamic that makes the pool large also makes casual, low-commitment dating the local default: worth knowing if you're specifically after something serious.
